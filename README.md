## 3. Problem Statement
Growing urban populations have created heavy traffic congestion, inefficient public transit routing, and unpredictable arrival times across city networks. Transport authorities and fleet operators often lack real-time, centralized visibility into live vehicle locations and commuter demand, making dynamic route adjustments difficult. This gap leads to higher fuel expenses, increased emissions, and longer wait times for passengers.

## 4. Introduction Brief
This project focuses on building a data analytics pipeline to collect, clean, and process urban transit and fleet telemetry data. By combining live vehicle tracking inputs with historical ridership patterns, the system aims to give operators a clearer picture of fleet distribution and traffic bottlenecks. The goal is to support better route scheduling, more reliable arrival time predictions, and improved overall transit efficiency.
